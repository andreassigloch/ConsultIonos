Stop hallucinating. Start producing.

Two questions are asked about implementation of AI in a business context: can I trust what it says, and can I trust what it produces? These four stages answer both.

Source: siglochconsulting.de/mein-ansatz
starting point

Smart search over documents

technical stage: unstructured data

AI finds passages that look relevant and summarises them. Faster than searching by hand — but it still guesses how the pieces fit together.

critical step

Stops hallucinations

technical stage: structured data

Suppliers, products, contracts and dependencies are captured as a verified network. The AI can only refer to connections that actually exist in your business.

trusted output

Output you can act on

technical stage: structured data + rule set

Compliance, compatibility, pricing rules are written down in a form the system actually applies. Anything the AI produces is checked — and rejected if it breaks a rule.

the payoff

Safe autonomous AI in business context

technical stage: self-correcting system

Outcomes flow back into the knowledge map. The system catches its own errors by checking the rules, tunes itself, and runs reliably without a person checking every step.

Stop hallucinations

AI invents things that sound right — until you give it a verified network.

Most so-called "hallucinations" are invented relationships: a supplier tied to the wrong product, a clause attributed to the wrong contract, a dependency that doesn't exist. Plain documents give the AI no way to know what's real.

Without this step

Plausible answers, no way to tell which are true. Every output needs a human check.

With this step

The AI can only reference connections that actually exist. If a link isn't there, it can't be claimed.

Technical answer: structured data — a knowledge map of your business.

Output you can act on

Plausible isn't the same as valid. Rules close the gap.

To let AI draft offers, configurations, contracts or specs, the result has to satisfy your business reality: regulations, compatibility, pricing logic. Rules turn "sounds reasonable" into "passes validation."

Without this step

AI proposes, a person manually checks. The bottleneck stays human.

With this step

AI proposes, the system validates, invalid options are blocked at source. Output is ready to use.

Technical answer: structured data + an executable rule set.

The bottom line

First, stop the AI from making things up. Then, make its output something you can act on. Once both hold, the AI starts running itself.
