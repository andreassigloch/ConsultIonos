Four stages — watch how scattered objects become connections, and how rules eliminate the connections (and objects) that don't belong.

Stage 1 / 4 Unstructured. Objects exist, but the AI can't see how they connect.
Rule for links
Every link must follow the business hierarchy. Stale or cross-type connections are removed.
↳ catches: phantom-supplier links, references to discontinued products, module → contract shortcuts
Rule for objects
Every object must reference something real. Phantom or orphan objects are removed.
↳ catches: suppliers that don't exist, products that have been retired
